Signal strength and switch point
  1. No target: turn the sensitivity up until the output turns on with nothing there, a false positive. Press Mark A.
  2. Target in: turn it down until the output turns off with the target there, a false negative. Press Mark B.
  3. Press Set midpoint: halfway between A and B gives the most room from both mistakes.
